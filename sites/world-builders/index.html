What we build

One team for the whole job.

You are not chasing a mason, then a roofer, then a tiler, and waiting on each one to turn up.

  • New houses

    Foundation to handover. One, two or three storeys.

  • Extensions

    An extra bedroom, a verandah, a second floor on a house that is already standing.

  • Renovations

    Older houses brought back up. Kitchens, bathrooms, floors, windows, wiring made safe.

  • Roofing

    Metal, tile and zinc. New roofs, and replacements after storm damage.

  • Concrete and block

    Footings, slabs, columns, retaining walls and boundary walls.

  • Repairs

    Cracks, leaks, settling, and finishes that did not last as long as they should have.

How it works

Five steps, and your part in each one.

It is your house and your money, so you are in the middle of this, not waiting at the end of it.

1

You get in touch

We doPick up, or call you back the same working day. We ask what you are building, which parish, and roughly what you have to spend.
You doTell us the parish and the rough size. If you have drawings, say so. If you have nothing but an idea and a piece of land, that is still a starting point.
2

We come and look

We doWalk the site with you. We measure, and we check the things that change a price later: access for a truck, the slope, water, and power.
You doBe there if you can. Half an hour on the site with you tells us more than a week of messages, and it is where most people change their mind about something.
3

You get the quote in writing

We doList it out by stage. Blocks, steel, cement, sand, labour, each with a quantity and a price. Not one lump sum at the bottom of a page.
You doRead it and question it. Ask what any line is for. If a stage is more than you expected, this is the point to cut it or change it, before anyone has bought anything.
4

We agree it and set a start date

We doPut the price, the payment stages and the start date in writing, so there is one document you can hold us to.
You doSign it and settle the first stage. Nothing is ordered and no one turns up on your land before that is agreed.
5

We build, and you can see it

We doSend photos as each stage closes. If a price moves, because steel went up or you asked for something different, you get the new figure in writing first.
You doCome to the site whenever you want. You do not need to give us notice. It is your land.
Our promise

Trust. Transparency. Quality.

Three words on our logo. Any builder can print them. Here is what each one has to mean on your job for us to keep saying it.

Trust

Mr. Ricardo Gayle runs this company. He is the one who comes to your site visit, and he is the one who picks up when something has gone wrong. You are not passed to an office that has never seen your land.

Transparency

Your quote shows quantities and prices line by line, so you can price any item yourself if you want to. Nothing gets bought on your job at a figure you have not seen and agreed.

Quality

Level, square and plumb checked before a stage is closed, because the cost of fixing it later lands on somebody. The photos we send are how you check we did it, not just how we advertise.
