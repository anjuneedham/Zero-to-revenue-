## Recommended: T1
- **Text:** BUILDING (off-white) / FROM ZERO (amber `#F2B01E`). 4 words max on the image. Alternative text: THE BOOTSTRAPPER EXPERIMENT (T2).
- **Placement:** text in the left two-thirds, top; UI card bottom-right; photo bottom-left. Keep 5% margins. Keep the key words out of the bottom-right corner where YouTube places the duration stamp.
- **Photo requirements:** 3000px or larger, daylight or a single soft key light, you at a desk or holding the tablet, looking at the screen or the camera, shoulders up, no logos, no other people, plain or slightly dark background. Remove the background and place it in the slot, or replace the slot with a full-bleed photo and darken it ~55%.
- **Colour treatment:** background `#0A0F1A`, vignette, amber highlight only on the second line. One accent colour, no gradients on the text.
- **Size and format:** 1280x720 (16:9), JPG or PNG under 2 MB.
- **Honesty check:** shows the real interface, no money, no chart, no result, no arrows or faces mimicking shock. The "DEMO DATA" label stays visible in the UI crop.
- **Readability at small size:** checked at 168x94 px (`graphics/small_size_check.png`): the headline stays legible on T1, T2 and T3.
