# Short-form extraction (each short = ONE complete idea; never a slice of the episode)
- Title · hook (first 1-2 s) · full narration (20-45 s) · storyboard (time, visual, shot, on-screen text) · captions · editing instructions · CTA · TikTok description · YouTube Shorts title · cover text
- Independent test: would someone who never saw the episode understand and get value from it?
- Honesty test: does every claim have evidence in the activity log or in a file?
- Put it in `editing-project/shorts_<N>.json`; build with `tools/produce.py short S1`.
