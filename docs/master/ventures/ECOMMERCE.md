# E-commerce (venture doc)
Candidates: `Kadeform-` (Shopify/Dawn, one hero product FlexBack Pro lumbar support, 446 files) and `NF-Fragrance-` (static Netlify site, five colognes, 24 files). Neither is fashion; no fashion repo found. *(handoff)* · stage: DECIDE · priority 3.

## Decision needed first
Founder picks THE brand. Logged as a decision in the OS. Recommendation: the one with a single hero product and an existing Shopify checkout (Kadeform-), unless the founder has real stock/supplier for the fragrances.

## Audit / risks
Unverified: live store status, supplier, margins, shipping, payment gateway for Jamaica. Paid ads can burn cash with no data. Product claims for a back-support product need care (health claims).

## MVP scope
One product page, working checkout, one supplier/fulfilment path, policies, tracking pixel.

## Roadmap
- [ ] Founder: choose brand; log decision
- [ ] Unit economics sheet: landed cost, price, margin, break-even CPA
- [ ] Confirm checkout + payment + shipping work end to end with a real test order
- [ ] Organic test (social posts, 14 days) before any ad spend
- [ ] One paid test with a hard cap set by the founder

## Metrics
visitors, add_to_cart_rate, orders, aov, cpa, gross_margin_pct (orders count as revenue only when paid and verified).

## Acceptance criteria
Test order completes; margin per order positive after ad cost for at least 10 orders.
**Kill criterion:** spend reaches the cap with CPA above break-even and no trend down → stop ads, log decision.
