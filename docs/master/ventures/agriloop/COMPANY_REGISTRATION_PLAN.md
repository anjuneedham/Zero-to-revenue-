# Agriloop: company registration plan (Jamaica) (DRAFT)

Prepared 30 Oct 2026 from general knowledge, NOT checked against current government pages. Fees, forms, thresholds and
portals change: confirm each step with the Companies Office of Jamaica (COJ) and Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) before paying.
Not legal or tax advice.

## 1. Choose the structure

| Option | Liability | Fits Agriloop? |
|---|---|---|
| Business name registration (sole trader or partnership) | Personal, unlimited | No. Cannot cleanly issue the partner's 10% ownership, and you carry every risk personally. |
| **Private company limited by shares (Ltd)** | Limited to the company | **Recommended.** Two shareholders, vesting on the partner's 10%, investors can come in later. |
| Public company | Limited | No. Not relevant at this stage. |

**Decision to make:** company name (check availability, avoid names that clash with "Agriloop"-like registered names),
directors (CEO plus optionally the partner), company secretary (can be the CEO or a service provider), registered office
address in Jamaica.

## 2. Steps and order

| # | Step | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Search and reserve the company name at COJ | CEO | Do first. Reservations expire, so file soon after. |
| 2 | Prepare Articles of Incorporation, director and secretary details, registered office, share structure | CEO (+ partner's details) | Start with 100 shares: CEO 90, partner 10 (subject to vesting agreement). |
| 3 | File incorporation at COJ and receive the Certificate of Incorporation | CEO | Self-filing may be possible; an attorney or company service provider can do it for a fixed fee. |
| 4 | Sign the Founder Agreement and record shares in the register of members | Both | Use `legal/FOUNDER_AGREEMENT.md`; vesting and leaver terms in writing. |
| 5 | Get the company's TRN and register for tax with TAJ | CEO | Income tax. Check GCT registration threshold (believed J$15M of taxable supplies per year; verify). |
| 6 | Open a business bank account | CEO | Usually needs the certificate, articles, TRNs, IDs and proof of address. |
| 7 | Register as a data controller with the Office of the Information Commissioner (OIC), if required under the Data Protection Act 2020 | CEO | Verify whether and when this applies to you. |
| 8 | Employer registrations (NIS, NHT, Education Tax, HEART) | CEO | Only when you hire employees. Confirm how the partner's 30% share is treated (contractor, shareholder, or employee). |
| 9 | Diary the annual return at COJ and tax filing dates | CEO | Missing these causes penalties. |

## 4. Costs (get real quotes)

Government fees, an attorney or provider fee (if used), bank minimums, and annual filing fees. Write the actual figures here
once quoted, and set aside a share of first income for the legal review.

## 5. Until the company exists

The founder agreement and terms should name the company that is being formed. If the beta must start first, operate as the CEO
personally for the invite-only beta only (no fees, no held funds), then assign everything to the company on incorporation.
Ask the attorney whether that assignment needs a short written transfer.
