The journey

Where this goes, and what you do at each stop.

The first week is about learning. After that it gets sharper every cycle, because each week starts from what the last one proved.

Week 1

Learn the game

Film the 7-day loop. Post every video on all four platforms. Log the numbers. Nobody expects winners yet.

Week 2

Find the first signals

Look at retention before views. Re-cut the hooks that held people. Drop nothing until you have tried a new hook, a stronger first shot and a shorter cut.

Weeks 3 and 4

Grow a content family

A winner becomes five or more variations. One problem that hits can feed weeks of videos.

Month 2 and on

Run the engine

You know which problems make builders and homeowners stop. That shapes what BUILDFlow builds next.

The first rule

Every video talks to one person.

Two audiences. Never mixed in one video. Orange is Builder, blue is Consumer, and that colour code runs through the whole page.

Audience A · 1 video a day

Builders and contractors

  • Run jobs, workers and customers, mostly in WhatsApp
  • Lose track of tasks and deadlines
  • Need more work and a way to be found
  • Want customers to trust them

"You have three jobs running at once..."

Audience B · 1 video a day

Homeowners and project owners

  • Need building, repairs or a renovation
  • Can't tell who to trust
  • Worry about delays and poor work
  • Want to see progress and know what to ask

"Need a contractor but have no idea who to trust?"

Testing

Change one thing at a time.

Every video tests one variable: the hook, the opening shot, the length, the topic, the CTA, the style, the curiosity, or where BUILDFlow shows up. Keep the core topic the same so the result means something.

Read these, in this order
  1. Retention
  2. Average watch %
  3. Shares
  4. Saves
  5. Comments
  6. Profile visits
  7. Follows

Views alone don't decide who won.

Don't just celebrate. Make a family.

When a video flops, try these before you drop the topic
  1. A new hook
  2. A stronger first visual
  3. A shorter version
  4. A different presentation
  5. A stronger curiosity loop

If several versions fail, move the topic down the list.

The loop that makes it all worth it.

Social media is not just marketing here. It's how we find out what to build. Tap a step.

Every idea has to answer these seven.

  • Who is this for? One audience.
  • What problem is it about? A real one from site or home.
  • Why would they stop? It names them.
  • What keeps them watching? An open question that's relevant.
  • What do they get? Something useful.
  • How does BUILDFlow relate? Truthfully.
  • What should they do next? Follow, save, share, comment. Rotate the ask.
Rules of the road

Stay true. It's the whole brand.

Contractors and homeowners can smell fake. Check every claim against the live app before you film it. Product state as of 25 Oct 2026.

Say it plainly, it exists in the beta
  • Customer and project management for contractors
  • A public contractor profile where customers can find them
Say "we're building", "we're testing" or "we're working on"
  • Push alerts for new leads
  • Messaging between customers and contractors
  • Paid billing and trials
  • Anything you haven't seen working on the phone
Fake testimonialsInvented usersMade-up revenueFake statistics"Thousands of contractors"Features that don't existEvery video an adLogo-first introsGeneric motivation
Make it local and real

Job sites, WhatsApp, delayed materials, workers waiting, masonry, plumbing, electrical, roofing, tiling, painting, extensions. Use Jamaican realities when they fit, but don't make every video Jamaica-only. BUILDFlow is meant to grow beyond one island. Phone footage, screen recordings, POV and handheld beat stock footage and polished ads.
