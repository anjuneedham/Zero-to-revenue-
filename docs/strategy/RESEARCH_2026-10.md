## Bounties
- Superteam Earn (reachable public API, 9 open listings on 25 Oct): rewards are prize POOLS shared among winners;
  competition ranged from 3 to 320 submissions; all 9 were HUMAN_ONLY (AI-produced work may be prohibited).
  Pays in USDC/USDG, typically on Solana, so a Solana address would be needed (Treasury watches EVM only).
- Open GitHub "bounty" label search is saturated with farmed repos (bounty-plaza, doomsday, frantic-board...).
- Web3 security (Immunefi, Sherlock, Cantina): highest ceilings (six to eight figures) but skill-gated; Sherlock
  requires a $250 USDC stake per report (conflicts with $0 capital). Only if the owner already has audit skills.
- Conclusion: bounties are a side channel for cash flow and credibility, not the engine.
