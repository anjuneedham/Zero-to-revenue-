Why we are doing this 10 min

Each says who the farmer is and what problem Agriloop fixes. Roles: you build, sell and onboard; she is the face, teaches and runs the community.

Outcome: one shared sentence that describes Agriloop.

Walk through the agreement 15 min

Open the Legal Pack. Decide together: how the 30% is calculated (platform fees after processing costs, or profit), vesting (suggested 24 months, 6-month cliff), and what happens if either leaves.

Outcome: every decision in the founder agreement has an answer, ready to sign once the company exists.

Show the app 10 min

She lists one product, you place an order from your phone, an invoice appears. Ask what confuses her.

Outcome: three things to fix, ranked.

Content plan 10 min

Agree on 3 videos a week. Hook ideas: price check, seedling to harvest, meet your parish farmer. Every video ends with one action: join through her link.

Outcome: the first three video ideas and posting days.

Name the first 10 10 min

She names 5 farmers or growers she knows in St. Mary or nearby, you name 5. Decide who contacts whom and by when.

Outcome: a list of 10 names with an owner for each.

Next actions 5 min

Each person leaves with three tasks and a date. Book the next call.

Outcome: a weekly rhythm: one call, one content day, one onboarding day.
